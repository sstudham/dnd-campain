# Locations

## Known Locations

* Blackford Road - site of Shorn's caravan ambush.
* Luskan - tied to Severed Whisper, Captain Braddok Ironhand, criminal trouble, dock activity, and the mayor's son.
* Sword Coast - broad region for the campaign's current movement.
* Kingdom of Many-Arrows - Throk's homeland and source of orc political tension.
* Brother Kai's monastery - quiet monastery on the north Sword Coast where Brother Kai was raised.
* Stormwreck Isle - current location; black rocks, undead dock attack, and strange dragon/rift resonance.
* Dragon's Rest - cloister above the Stormwreck Isle dock; the party is walking uphill from the dock after killing zombies, and kobolds are the immediate contact.
* Glittervein - mine where Floyd GoldSeeker was trapped in a collapse with his crew; Floyd survived after a golden ray of light shone through a tiny opening onto his heart.
* Clifftop Observatory - Stormwreck Isle ruin Runara pointed the party toward after the Compass Rose. The party completed the approach, D1 Overlook, and D2 Rotunda Ruins; another dragon is trapped beneath or inside the tower.
* D1 Overlook - Clifftop Observatory overlook with two marble dragon statues veined with gold; Floyd GoldSeeker activated the moonstone key here to create the magical bridge.
* D2 Rotunda Ruins - ruined Observatory rotunda with a golden astronomical sculpture and five dragon effigies; Mek died here, Minn survived unless later contradicted, all eight stirges were killed, and Floyd GoldSeeker reached Level 2.
