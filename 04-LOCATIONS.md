# Locations

## Known Locations

* Blackford Road - site of Shorn's caravan ambush.
* Luskan - tied to Severed Whisper, Captain Braddok Ironhand, criminal trouble, dock activity, and the mayor's son.
* Sword Coast - broad region for the campaign's current movement.
* Kingdom of Many-Arrows - Throk's homeland and source of orc political tension.
* Brother Kai's monastery - quiet monastery on the north Sword Coast where Brother Kai was raised.
* Stormwreck Isle - current location; black rocks, undead dock attack, and strange dragon/rift resonance. Canonical Session 15 ending: after the Observatory crisis, the party leaves Stormwreck Isle by ship while Minn, Myla, Runara, and Aidron watch from the cliffs.
* Dragon's Rest - cloister above the Stormwreck Isle dock and the party's current home base. Canonical Session 15 ending: Aidron returns alive, Myla receives Mek's body, the kobolds grieve him, and Runara and Aidron give the party the durable lead to follow the dragons.
* Glittervein - mine where Floyd GoldSeeker was trapped in a collapse with his crew; Floyd survived after a golden ray of light shone through a tiny opening onto his heart.
* Clifftop Observatory - Stormwreck Isle ruin Runara pointed the party toward after the Compass Rose. The party completed the approach, D1 Overlook, D2 Rotunda Ruins, D4, and reached D5. Sparkrender was killed in D5. Canonical Session 15 outcome: the Observatory destabilizes after Sparkrender's death, Aidron is freed, and what awakens there reaches outward strongly enough for other dragons to feel it.
* D1 Overlook - Clifftop Observatory overlook with two marble dragon statues veined with gold; Floyd GoldSeeker activated the moonstone key here to create the magical bridge.
* D2 Rotunda Ruins - ruined Observatory rotunda with a golden astronomical sculpture and five dragon effigies; Mek died here, Minn survived, all eight stirges were killed, and Floyd GoldSeeker reached Level 2.
* D4 - Observatory chamber where the party recovered a clue-bearing book or journal. Throk was poisoned while opening it. Brother Kai accessed the treasure and the party received 2 potions of resistance and 1 minor healing potion.
* D5 - chamber where the party immediately attacked Sparkrender. Pat Benatar struck him hard with her shortbow, and Throk killed him with Eldritch Blast. The session ended at his death. D5 remains unsearched in played table history. Behind-the-screen canon: Sparkrender's death destabilizes the Observatory; four scholar statues aligned toward the Dragon of Dawn open the lower route while blue corruption tries to restore the old pattern.
* D6 - lower Observatory area not yet entered in played table history. Behind-the-screen canon: Aidron is a living bronze dragon trapped here in shifting stone and corrupted golden lines and is freed during the canonical Session 15 climax.

## Important Details

* Minn remains near the magical bridge as lookout in played table history unless later clarified.
* Minn asked the party to return Mek's body to Dragon's Rest.
* Canonical Session 15 ending: Minn carries Mek back to Dragon's Rest and says, "He came home."
* Canonical Session 15 departure: the party sails away the next morning; Stormwreck Isle disappears behind them before Pat Benatar's scale warms and a distant golden light and vast winged shadow appear beyond the horizon.

## Unvisited or Rumored

* The lower Observatory beyond D5 remains unvisited in played table history.
* An unnamed human settlement seen through the Observatory's distant golden connection, threatened beneath a torn sky by a dragon-shaped shadow.
* The location beyond the horizon associated with the golden light and winged shadow seen after the party leaves Stormwreck is unknown.
