You believe that because we are dragons, we must know what is happening.

We do not.

We feel disturbances. Changes in places that have endured longer than your towns. What happened on Stormwreck reached outward. Here, beneath your mill, you found old workings damaged and disturbed.

I want to understand what those signs have in common. That is why your plate interests me. That is why I want to examine the crystal. And why your account matters.

But I do not know what is causing this. Neither our age nor our power has given us that answer.

Do not mistake my interest for certainty.

You have walked through places I have not. You have witnessed things I have only sensed from a distance.

So tell me what happened. Begin with what you actually saw. Perhaps, between us, we may find a question worth pursuing.
