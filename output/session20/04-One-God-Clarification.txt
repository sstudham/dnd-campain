The One God.

You have followed that name as though it must belong to the power behind all this.

It does not.

The world gives frightened people reasons to doubt. A prayer behaves strangely. An old stone wakes. Something that should be impossible happens before witnesses. Then someone steps forward and says: our god did this. Only we understand. Follow us, and you will be safe.

That is the opportunity these people have seized.

Their leaders take real events and build authority around them. Fear brings followers. Relief brings gratitude. Gratitude becomes obedience. Those who claim to speak for the only true god acquire a great deal of power over everyone who believes them.

But do not confuse every believer with the people exploiting that belief. Some are frightened. Some are grieving. Some genuinely wish to help. The woman tending the wounded in Greenest may sincerely believe every word she says. A sincere healer can still be wrong about where her stone's power comes from.

The relief is real. Her explanation is another matter.

The worshiping stone you encountered drew prayer toward something other than a god. That does not make its destination the One God. It makes the destination a question.

The pale stone in Greenest is another object. Its relief does not prove the same origin, or the same purpose. It works through something already present beneath the town. Calling that a miracle does not explain the old channels, or the damage around them.

You have seen that older workmanship yourselves. Beneath the mill. On Stormwreck. Those things were there long before these preachers began claiming answers.

The One God movement is taking advantage of the crisis. It is not the answer to what caused it. Its people can exploit damaged places, misuse what they find, and make suffering worse. That makes them dangerous. It does not make them the architects of the wound.

And Frulam's Cult of the Dragon is a separate concern. Do not give every person with a doctrine the same master merely because your paths have crossed theirs.

You could expose every false promise and scatter every preacher, and the deeper damage would remain.

I do not know what is causing that damage. Neither do the dragons who have sensed it.

Keep helping the people caught in it. Challenge those who profit from their fear. But if you want to understand what is happening to the world... follow the evidence beyond the people claiming to have explained it.
