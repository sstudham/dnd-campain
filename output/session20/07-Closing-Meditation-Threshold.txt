You settle into the circle.

Lennithon sits among you in a human shape. Beside him, the frail traveler steadies his breathing. For the first time since you found him, he is no longer attempting this alone.

Candlelight warms your closed eyelids. Somewhere beyond the cushions, four silent guards stand watch.

You remember the monastery. The impossible distance. The wizard who warned that more than one world was in danger.

One breath. Then another.

The cavern's echoes grow faint. The weight of your armor, the ache of your wounds, the stone beneath you... all begin to feel farther away.

Then you see the golden lines of the Observatory.

They pass beneath the broken channels of the mill. For a moment, two distant places overlap. You recognize the same unsettling rhythm in each: the trembling you felt, the pattern you could never quite hold in your mind.

This time, you can follow it a little farther.

You begin to understand why the dragon needed your stories. Each of you remembers something the others missed. Together, those fragments form a connection none of you could see alone.

Pat-Benatar, the mark upon your scale lingers in that fading light. Those entrusted to keep watch. The words remain with you, and the question beneath them: watch over what?

The answer does not come.

Instead, the familiar images open into something wider.

Colors without a sky. Distances without a horizon. The sensation of standing before countless possible places, while somewhere far behind you, your bodies remain seated in a quiet cavern.

You reach toward the memory of the wizard.

Whether he hears you, you cannot tell.

But something changes.

The circle, the candles, and the dragon's voice recede beyond your senses. Around you, another reality begins to take shape.

You have crossed the threshold.

What waits beyond it is about to become your story.
