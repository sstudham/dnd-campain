Come closer. Let me look at you.

You have brought such interesting toys into my home. The plate... and that little crystal.

Tell me. Where did you find them? What happened when you disturbed the place where they rested?

And will you share your toys with me? You may keep your hands upon them while I examine them. For now, I ask to see.

But objects tell only part of a story.

What have you seen? What have you heard? What has happened to you that you cannot explain?

Perhaps you possess something more valuable than anything in your packs. Knowledge.

Will you share that with me as well?

Tell me what you wish to know in return. Let us discover whether we can be useful to one another.
