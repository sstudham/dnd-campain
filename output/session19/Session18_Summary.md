### Actual outcomes
* Severed Whisper openly faced the two diversion wagons at the bridge, four hired raiders total. His opening natural 1 missed; a later attack for 12 damage dropped one. He took 8 damage, retreated up the oak, and the raiders fetched axes and chopped at the tree. His crossbow dealt 8 to one raider, leaving that target at 1 HP. The three abandoned the tree and ran into town. Severed Whisper descended, pursued and killed the wounded raider with a crossbow shot. The other two turned back; he took another 6 damage. Throk's friendly troops joined him. Scott explicitly confirmed Severed Whisper killed another with his rapier (17 to hit, 12 damage in Otter) with friends nearby, and the last was captured alive. Final wagon-group outcome: three dead, one captive. Current HP remains unconfirmed despite 14 recorded damage and continued activity.
* Pat-Benatar approached the two lookout scouts with twelve guards. Group stealth failed (16 against 17); both scouts surrendered to the superior numbers. One threw down and extinguished the lantern; her catch attempt failed (13 against DC 15). She later repaired it (10 against DC 10). She sent guards toward the mill and the previously unguarded road, retaining three initially. She located the eastern group with a 22 against stealth 2. Her two flashes followed by steady light were intended as an allied danger warning; the DM ruled the sustained portion released the enemy advance. Preserve that actual ruling separately from the older stricter written trigger. She considered executing the bound scouts, then explicitly chose to leave them alive and bound and head toward the mill. No escape or later execution was resolved.
* Throk distributed six guards along the west path, captured an enemy messenger by force and ordered him bound. His Insight 4 did not verify the claim that the messenger was alone. Throk then summoned a ghostly hawk familiar for the first time through will and an unexplained patron connection; he experienced its sight. No patron identity was revealed, and he did not physically transform into a bird. After guards moved away, the unguarded messenger broke his bonds on a 15 and fled. The hawk saw him escape. Throk's Eldritch Blast 7 missed. Two guards were ordered to pursue and kill the messenger; no pursuit outcome or message delivery was resolved. Throk and his remaining guards moved to support Severed Whisper, then converged toward the mill.
* Brother Kai and Floyd GoldSeeker supervised the work crews. Several early investigations failed. Brother Kai's bracing design succeeded with Insight 23; this did not itself complete construction. Floyd GoldSeeker split labor equally between shoring and digging, then succeeded on the actual shoring (18) and digging (16) checks. A wrong-die first roll was corrected, not an additional structural failure. Timber shoring held. A barricade was built across the entrance (Persuasion 16) while excavation continued. A later effort to intimidate workers into accelerating did not succeed.
* Enemies reached the barricade. Brother Kai used fire breath through a small opening. Six targets were resolved with advantaged saves at final stated DC 11: one warrior took 6 fire damage; the other warrior and four laborers took 3 each; two others were outside the blast. No first-wave casualties were explicitly established. The barricade was scorched and threatened but not destroyed. Friendly messages and reinforcements were discussed; do not count all planned arrivals as already completed.
* Brother Kai struck the excavation wall for 13 damage and opened a small dark cavity. A fist-sized pink crystal rested on four prongs/gold channels; the front pair led toward the plate position, and the rear pair entered the wall. Brother Kai lifted the crystal with his claws and held it. A pulse traveled along the channels, the earth briefly shook and dust fell. The shored structure held. No universal repair, identified function or magical PC benefit was established. The plate had been handed to the mill group before this scene; exact carrier and socket placement remain unconfirmed.
* Severed Whisper reached the outside of the mill, visibly bloodied, and intimidated the attackers with a furious feline roar (16). Rank-and-file attackers scattered; a commander remained. Later closing staging explicitly placed a further enemy wagon group behind the forward guards. Its relationship to the routed people and exact roster were not settled; do not erase either the rout or the final staging.
