BALANCED OPENING PROPOSAL
Use a short confrontation over control, surrender and safe extraction, not an obligatory full-force slaughter. Start with the remaining commander plus the later wagon force. Add no reinforcements or surprise boss powers. Do not restore enemies who fled.
If the later wagon has no settled roster, proposed cap: TWO fighting escorts and FOUR frightened workers. These are a proposed subset/interpretation of the unresolved wagon group, not six newly discovered people. If actual closing roster is clarified differently, use that count and keep workers noncombatant.
Existing Hired Raider reference for genuinely uninjured escorts: AC12, maximumHP9, speed30ft, club+3, 1d4+1. The commander uses the same existing campaign record unless Scott establishes another one; no multiattack or new ranged power. Preserve prior wounds: two first-wave warriors took6 and3 fire respectively; the surviving commander's identity among them is unresolved. If this is that commander, verify whether3 or6HP remain rather than resetting to9.
Workers drop tools, take cover, flee or surrender when clearly outmatched. Keep the route out usable. They can bargain and answer ordinary work-order questions; they do not know hidden lore.
Front four friendly guards form one blocking group: contain the wagon escorts and keep pressure off Severed Whisper. Rear friendly group secures retreat/captives when it actually arrives. Resolve matching NPC clashes as one group exchange per round; use existing guard records if individual attacks become material. No unearned extra PC actions or blanket HP buffs.
Let each PC choose one important action: Brother Kai safeguards crystal/controls choke; Floyd GoldSeeker protects workers and manages exit; Severed Whisper can withdraw to guards, negotiate or confront commander; Throk directs hawk/guards; Pat-Benatar decides interception/surrender terms. These are opportunities, not assigned actions.
Commander can surrender, bargain for a route out, or fight according to actual choices. A surrender is a valid conclusion; no required last stand. Ryan's absence is handled by grouped NPC turns, not by inventing his NPC or increasing lethality.
