SESSION 19 MENTAL REHEARSAL
Private DM preparation, not predetermined events. No new companion, recovery or knowledge assumed. Scott runs NPCs without Ryan.

Begin in the same moment in which Session 18 stopped. Picture the mill chamber, its timbers holding after a brief tremor. Brother Kai has the pink crystal. Floyd GoldSeeker stands nearby. The wooden entrance barricade is scorched, and beyond it the commander has not yet given up. Severed Whisper has reached the opposite side of that confrontation, bloodied and furious. Four friendly guards stand farther down the approach. Beyond them is the later wagon group. Throk and Pat-Benatar are coming up behind it with allies.

Before the first declaration, settle the numbers that matter. Ask Severed Whisper for actual current HP. Fourteen damage was spoken during play, but old sheets are not a current total. Confirm the surviving commander's wounds and the later wagon's roster. The map marks order, not a new initiative result. Do not spend ten minutes recreating every guard's turn. Let friendly groups contain matching opposition and let player choices decide the sharp points.

Imagine the commander's position. He can hear people inside, see Severed Whisper outside, and realize that his force has lost coherence. He can fight, demand a way out or accept surrender terms. No story requires him to die. A practical offer may end the encounter faster than another round of attacks. If battle continues, preserve damage and escape opportunities. Workers are frightened people with tools, not a new hidden squad of veterans. The later wagon is not an excuse to return everyone who fled.

Let the five PCs see a useful choice. Brother Kai can protect what he holds or manage the narrow opening. Floyd GoldSeeker can prevent the rescue operation from becoming another dangerous worksite. Severed Whisper can accept guard cover instead of remaining the lone target. Throk can decide how to use the hawk without losing awareness of his own body. Pat-Benatar can turn her successful lookout position into terms, interception or custody. These are invitations, not scripted actions.

When danger ends, lower the pace. The mill's water is still moving. Workers need instructions, injured people need care, and captives need a destination. Ask who is carrying the control plate and how the crystal is protected. The pink crystal's appearance is now a played fact. Its old prepared pressure-witness design differs; do not pretend the players already learned a technical explanation. A later experiment can reveal a limited observation after that background is reconciled.

If the group chooses the sanctuary, show the patients and the actual care before the magical question. Merra's relief is real within its narrow limits. Eadyan wants a method people can trust. A safe controlled observation can make relief and structural strain visible together. A complete care plan allows isolation; monitored use remains possible. Neither route repairs every damaged channel or settles a religion. Give the players time to choose a responsibility they can leave in good hands.

If they want to follow the dragon lead, ask where their directions came from. Use the messenger, docket, tracks or another earned source. They can choose to rest, but it must be an actual declared rest with recovery resolved. Their equipment and burdens are theirs to assign. Ryan is absent and no NPC bond was established, so no sixth traveler appears automatically. Use five-PC or terrain art for departure.

The northern rendezvous need not become combat. A shadowed dragon can ask what they want to discuss. He cannot accuse them of concealed inventory he has not seen. His interest is independent of Frulam. A bounded exchange, copied observations or a refusal can all move the story. Give one supported connection and one question sharpened by an answer. Do not fill the silence with the rift's ultimate explanation or Throk's patron identity.

End where the choices genuinely lead. If the mill and care decisions take the night, finish with a responsible handoff and a route, not a rushed dragon speech. If they reach conversation, let them choose the next objective: cult activity, local repair or a limited northern observation. Record the promises and actual custody. The next session should open from what they chose, not from the unused branch of this rehearsal.
