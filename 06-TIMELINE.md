# Timeline

## Past Events

* Brother Kai was given to a quiet monastery on the north Sword Coast at age 5.
* Brother Kai was sent wandering at age 23 to seek enlightenment.
* Pat Benatar grew up in a dragonborn clan but was treated as strange because she appeared mostly human.
* Pat Benatar recovered stolen clan goods as a young adult but still felt more useful than accepted.
* Pat Benatar left her clan and learned shipping, docks, warehouses, routes, favors, and port life.
* Severed Whisper arrived in Luskan by ship from the Tabaxi homeland with Captain Braddok Ironhand.
* Throk left the Kingdom of Many-Arrows after orc political conflict.
* Floyd GoldSeeker was a master surveyor for the Goldseeker clan, known for finding rich veins of ore.
* Floyd GoldSeeker was trapped with his crew when the Glittervein mine collapsed.
* Floyd and his crew spent three days trying to dig free; his crewmates died as the air ran out.
* Near death, Floyd saw a pebble fall from above and a golden ray of light shine through a tiny opening onto his heart.
* Floyd used his renewed strength and pickaxe to break through and escape Glittervein; he believes this was divine intervention.

## Current Events

* Shorn's caravan was ambushed on the Blackford Road by orcs led by Grusk and Morghul.
* Three young orcs and their wolf were killed.
* Grusk and Morghul escaped.
* Throk fought beside the caravan, was wounded, earned trust, and joined the journey north toward Luskan.
* The cult of "the one god" appeared among normal worshipers.
* A worshiping stone pulled prayer into something other than a god.
* A separation event occurred.
* Severed Whisper became entangled with the party through Luskan, Captain Braddok Ironhand, the odd stone, and criminal trouble.
* Pat Benatar's dragon-blood mystery became tied to a scale and clan symbol.
* Stormwreck Isle / Dragon's Rest is now the current location; unresolved threats and mysteries there remain active.
* The party returned from the Compass Rose to Dragon's Rest, completed a long rest, and has been pointed toward the Clifftop Observatory.

## Future Clocks

* Grusk and Morghul remain unresolved.
* The rift problem behind the cult remains unresolved.
