# Timeline

## Past Events

* Brother Kai was given to a quiet monastery on the north Sword Coast at age 5.
* Brother Kai was sent wandering at age 23 to seek enlightenment.
* Pat Benatar grew up in a dragonborn clan but was treated as strange because she appeared mostly human.
* Pat Benatar recovered stolen clan goods as a young adult but still felt more useful than accepted.
* Pat Benatar left her clan and learned shipping, docks, warehouses, routes, favors, and port life.
* Severed Whisper arrived in Luskan by ship from the Tabaxi homeland with Captain Braddok Ironhand.
* Throk left the Kingdom of Many-Arrows after orc political conflict.
* Floyd GoldSeeker was a master surveyor for the Goldseeker clan, known for finding rich veins of ore.
* Floyd GoldSeeker was trapped with his crew when the Glittervein mine collapsed.
* Floyd and his crew spent three days trying to dig free; his crewmates died as the air ran out.
* Near death, Floyd saw a pebble fall from above and a golden ray of light shine through a tiny opening onto his heart.
* Floyd used his renewed strength and pickaxe to break through and escape Glittervein; he believes this was divine intervention.

* Shorn's caravan was ambushed on the Blackford Road by orcs led by Grusk and Morghul.
* Three young orcs and their wolf were killed.
* Grusk and Morghul escaped.
* Throk fought beside the caravan, was wounded, earned trust, and joined the journey north toward Luskan.

* Runara gave Floyd GoldSeeker the moonstone key.
* Floyd activated the moonstone key at D1 Overlook and created the magical bridge to the Observatory ruins.
* Mek died during the D2 Rotunda Ruins stirge encounter; Minn survived.
* Floyd GoldSeeker reached Level 2 at the Clifftop Observatory.
* The party gave Minn gold for his help and promised to return Mek's body to Dragon's Rest.
* In D4, Throk was poisoned while opening the clue-bearing book or journal.
* Brother Kai accessed the D4 treasure, and the party received 2 potions of resistance and 1 minor healing potion.
* The party entered D5 and immediately attacked Sparkrender without first investigating or negotiating.
* Pat Benatar struck Sparkrender hard with her shortbow.
* Throk killed Sparkrender with Eldritch Blast.
* Sparkrender's death destabilized the Observatory.
* The party solved the four-scholar / Dragon of Dawn puzzle and opened the route below the Observatory.
* During the puzzle, Pat Benatar studied her clan-marked scale against the Observatory's ancient pattern with a DC 19 result.
* Pat learned that the mark on her scale is related to the Observatory's ancient golden pattern, that the pattern predates her clan, and that her clan likely inherited or adopted the older draconic mark rather than creating it.
* The party descended below D5 and freed Aidron, the living bronze dragon trapped in shifting stone and corrupted golden lines.
* The Observatory's acute forced pattern broke.
* The party returned Mek's body to Dragon's Rest.
* Myla and the kobolds grieved Mek; Minn said, "He came home."
* Aidron returned alive to Dragon's Rest and reunited with Runara.
* Runara and Aidron told the party that what awakened in the Observatory reached outward and that other dragons would have felt it.
* Runara told the party that if they want to understand the wound, they should follow the dragons. Aidron warned them not to interpret that as advice to trust dragons.
* The party sailed away from Stormwreck Isle.
* After Stormwreck disappeared behind them, Pat Benatar's scale became warm.
* A distant point of golden light appeared beyond the horizon, moved, and was accompanied by a vast winged shadow. The scale then cooled.
* Act 1, Scene 2 is complete. The next arc is Act 1, Scene 3.
