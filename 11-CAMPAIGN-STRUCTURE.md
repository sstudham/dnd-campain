# Campaign Structure

Planning structure, not canon unless later confirmed in canon files.

## Long-Term Campaign Structure

* This is a long-term D\&D campaign emphasizing collaborative storytelling and collective worldbuilding in a world loosely inspired by Faerun.
* The campaign may span a decade or more of real-time play.
* The campaign is expected to include 10+ Acts total.
* Each Act may last \~1 year of real-time play.
* Each Act may include 4-6 Scenes.
* Each Scene may last 3-5 Sessions.
* Character progression is intentionally slow, usually 1-3 levels per Act, to emphasize story and worldbuilding over rapid power growth.
* DM duties may rotate by Scene or Act to share creative load and bring different perspectives.

## Act Structure

* Acts are major plot arcs defined collaboratively before play.
* Each Act should draw on group input for major events, villains, and themes.
* Each Act should feel like a full campaign arc, but with restrained leveling.

## Scene Structure

* Scenes are smaller arcs within Acts.
* Scenes usually last 3-5 Sessions.
* High-level Scene outlines are created before the Act begins.
* Detailed encounters, NPCs, and hooks are prepared shortly before each Scene to preserve flexibility.

## Possible Campaign Premise - Not Yet Canon

* One possible cause is the destruction of a powerful artifact tied to Chaos or Entropy, causing instability across realms.
* The ancient polytheistic pantheons may be under siege by a viral, ultra-violent monotheistic faith.
* As gods and followers fall, planar barriers may erode further, allowing entropy and chaos to spread.
