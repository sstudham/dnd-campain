## Mill contents defined — approved DM canon September 30, 2026

**Pressure witness:** forearm-long smoky crystal spindle, roughly ten pounds, three old bronze ribs and retaining clips, one still amber line. A removable reference component retaining this vault's last stable local pressure reading, not an egg, soul, weapon or second plate. Support, release clips, lift and pad for safe recovery. No HP, healing, spell, combat or general PC benefit.

If the plate is actually brought close, a second offset amber line briefly compares the stored local reading with a present reading. It does not repair the existing physical break or open the sealed arch. Removing the witness leaves the damaged architecture in place. Adjacent shored masonry provides the prepared access, not a newly enormous machine.

No retrieval, test or party custody has occurred yet. Merra's receiver retains all existing bounded effects below: genuine relief for three patients, no HP/slots, paired ten-minute strain, reversible bench isolation with ordinary care. This component does not replace or identify any earlier stone. Local receiver isolation can reduce one load; wider repair remains necessary.

## Greenest Objects — Approved DM Canon September 9, 2026

Background remains DM-only unless Session 16's discoveries and custody updates below explicitly establish party knowledge.

* Control plate — dark charcoal stone with gold/brass draconic-looking inlay; palm-wide, rectangular, with fresh pry marks, branching grooves reaching its edges and no obvious writing. Earlier “brass” descriptions refer to the inlay. The first mill team sought both the contents behind the arch and this intact portable component. Unable to open the damaged mechanism, they extracted the plate; the party intercepted it before delivery. It fitted flush in the party’s test, restored faint vibration and amber pulses, and remained removable. Every pulse stopped at the existing physical break; the arch stayed shut. The party removed it again; **Throk holds it at Session 16 close**. DM-only: Lennithon wants both this plate and the unspecified contents beyond the arch, for unrevealed reasons. The network still wants the plate transferred north through intermediaries. Spade’s separate brute-force breach needs no plate. If identified in enemy hands, raiders seek to recover it; ordinary infiltration requires concealment. No automatic surrender or offscreen theft.
* Merra's pale relief stone — a distinct local One God receiver, not silently identified with the earlier damaged worshiping stone or Severed Whisper's odd stone. While seated above its channel, within 20 feet it eases pain and labored breathing for three injured, conscious civilians. It grants no HP, temporary HP, spell slots, immunity or general PC buff. Every ten fictional minutes its pulse coincides with relief and new dust from a widening nearby crack. It routes power or prayer through a non-divine receiver; this is not proof of the cult's theology.
* Moving the stone onto an ordinary wooden bench away from the channel for one cycle stops relief, pulsing and new dust together. Fresh air, water, bandages and ordinary care keep the patients stable. Replacing it restores the paired effect. Breaking it ends its local effect and loses its potential use, without an explosion or instant deaths. Disabling it reduces one load but leaves the wider vault needing repair.
* Unsigned work order — recovered/read by the party by their Session 16 debate at the arch; exact pickup location and individual holder were not reported. Text: “Under the mill. Brass plate intact. Do not turn the channels. North road, then the chalk cut. Deliver before dawn.” The wording establishes the raiders' intended delivery deadline, not automatic success or a rescue death timer.

* Cavern map — consulted at the mayor's home/town hall library. The party understood the route to the plate's original location beneath the mill and used that knowledge while hallucinating. Taking or owning the original map was not confirmed; labels and geometry added by generated art are not authoritative.
* Boots stuffed monkey — mundane toy perceived as Boots from Dora the Explorer, apparently alive and guiding the party during the LSD hallucination. No actual guide, animation or magic is established. Individual custody of the toy is unspecified.
* Fireball — Pat Benatar drank the cinnamon whisky in the library scene. No special magical effect or numeric inventory is established.

The sanctuary's pale stone was seen during the arrival scene; its removal, destruction, transfer or testing was not reported. Do not infer its final placement or the patients' care arrangements from unused prep.
