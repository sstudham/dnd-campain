# Items

## Party Items / Notable Objects

* Odd stone - tied to Luskan, Severed Whisper, and the party's larger trouble; current holder/status unknown.
* Worshiping stone - cracked or damaged cult object that pulled prayer into something other than a god.
* Pat Benatar's scale - gold-copper scale marked with Pat Benatar's clan symbol; carried by Pat Benatar, reacts to dragonborn, and pointed toward Stormwreck. In Session 15, Pat studied the scale against the Clifftop Observatory's ancient pattern with a DC 19 result. She learned that the mark is related to the same underlying pattern built into the Observatory's golden lines, that the Observatory and pattern predate her clan, and that the scale became distinctly warm as though recognizing or reacting to the pattern. Pat can reasonably conclude that her clan inherited or adopted a much older draconic symbol or pattern connected to ancient draconic sites. She did not learn exactly what she is, why she has the scale, the full history of her clan's connection, or the ultimate meaning of the pattern. After the party sailed away from Stormwreck Isle, the scale became warm but not hot or painful; a distant point of golden light moved beyond the horizon while a vast winged shadow passed beneath or behind it, then the scale cooled.
* Pat Benatar's sealed box - opened for Pat Benatar; source of the scale and connected to her unusual origin.
* Cart of potatoes - valuable cargo that first drew Severed Whisper toward the party.
* Floyd GoldSeeker's warhammer - cleric weapon modeled after his old mining pickaxe; visually and emotionally tied to Glittervein, excavation, survival, and protecting companions.
* Moonstone key - key Runara gave to Floyd GoldSeeker; Floyd used it at D1 Overlook to activate the marble dragon statues and create the magical bridge into the Clifftop Observatory ruins.
* Golden astronomical sculpture - ancient Observatory instrument tied to timing, pressure, celestial movement, dragon death, hidden lines of power, and the wider draconic pattern. Its full original purpose remains unresolved.
* D4 book or journal - recovered by the party at the Clifftop Observatory. It contained the clue used to solve the D5 four-scholar / Dragon of Dawn puzzle and open the route toward the lower Observatory.
* Potions of resistance - 2 obtained from D4. Resistance type unspecified. The party has not yet identified, distributed, or consumed them unless later table notes clarify otherwise.
* Minor healing potion - 1 obtained from D4. Exact mechanics unspecified. Do not treat it as a standard Potion of Healing unless Scott confirms that equivalence.

## Unclaimed or Unknown

* Sparkrender's hoard - present in D5. Its final claimed/distributed status was not supplied in the Session 15 update; do not assume ownership or distribution without a table note.
