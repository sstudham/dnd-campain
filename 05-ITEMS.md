# Items

## Party Items / Notable Objects

* Odd stone - tied to Luskan, Severed Whisper, and the party's larger trouble.
* Worshiping stone - cracked or damaged cult object that pulled prayer into something other than a god.
* Pat Benatar's scale - gold-copper scale marked with Pat Benatar's clan symbol; connected to her dragon-blood mystery.
* Pat Benatar's sealed box - box connected to the scale and Pat Benatar's unusual origin.
* Cart of potatoes - valuable cargo that first drew Severed Whisper toward the party.
