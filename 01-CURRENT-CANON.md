## Campaign Premise

* This is a long-form D&D campaign in a Faerun-inspired world.
* The campaign currently involves weakening planar barriers, strange magical anomalies, and a religious/planar threat
* The world has suffered, or will suffer, a major catastrophe that weakens the barriers between planes.
* The rift is a wound in reality.
* The rift's full nature, cause, and limits are not yet known.
* The rift is not simply a portal. It represents damage to the structure of existence.
* The gods are still real, but the rift is causing them to interact with the world slightly differently.
* Space, time, magic, and religion are beginning to fray.
* The problem is larger than the party's local reality.
* The party was singled out during a shared monastery vision and shown that the crisis is larger than any one cult, town, god, or broken temple.
* A being of impossible power reached across realities and summoned the party's minds to another plane or higher-dimensional reality.
* The being projected itself as a powerful ancient spellcaster because its true reality did not fit ordinary three-dimensional understanding.
* The being warned that time, space, religion, magic, prayer, memory, and reality itself are beginning to fray across planes and realities.
* Even this being did not know the source of the damage, making the crisis more frightening and urgent.
* The monastery vision is one of the party's main reasons to understand that they are special and tied to the central campaign conflict.
