# Current Canon

## Campaign Premise

* This is a long-form D&D campaign in a Faerun-inspired world.
* The campaign currently involves weakening planar barriers, strange magical anomalies, and a religious/planar threat
* The world has suffered, or will suffer, a major catastrophe that weakens the barriers between planes.
* The rift is a wound in reality.
* The rift's full nature, cause, and limits are not yet known.
* The rift is not simply a portal. It represents damage to the structure of existence.
* The gods are still real, but the rift is causing them to interact with the world slightly differently.
* Space, time, magic, and religion are beginning to fray.
* The problem is larger than the party's local reality.
* The party was singled out during a shared monastery vision and shown that the crisis is larger than any one cult, town, god, or broken temple.
* A being of impossible power reached across realities and summoned the party's minds to another plane or higher-dimensional reality.
* The being projected itself as a powerful ancient spellcaster because its true reality did not fit ordinary three-dimensional understanding.
* The being warned that time, space, religion, magic, prayer, memory, and reality itself are beginning to fray across planes and realities.
* Even this being did not know the source of the damage, making the crisis more frightening and urgent.
* The monastery vision is one of the party's main reasons to understand that they are special and tied to the central campaign conflict.

## Established Facts

* The party began with Level 0 individual backstory play.
* Early omens suggested weakening planar barriers, including flickering magic, strange dreams, and minor planar anomalies.
* The cult of "the one god" has appeared among normal worshipers.
* The cult's worshiping stone was pulling prayer into something other than a god.
* During deep meditation at the monastery, the party shared a vision in which their minds were pulled beyond the world they knew.
* They were contacted by a being of impossible power from another plane or higher-dimensional reality.
* The being appeared as an ancient spellcaster only because that was a form the party could understand.
* The being warned that the crisis was not local: not one cult, one town, one god, or one broken temple.
* Across realities, the structures of time, space, religion, magic, prayer, memory, and reality itself are beginning to fray.
* The being did not know what was causing the damage.
* The monastery vision is canonically one of the major hooks showing the party that they are special and connected to the central crisis.
* Throk joined Shorn's caravan after the Blackford Road ambush.
* Grusk and Morghul escaped and remain unresolved.
* Pat Benatar's dragon-blood mystery is connected to a scale and clan symbol.
* Severed Whisper is tied to Luskan, Captain Braddok Ironhand, an odd stone, and criminal trouble in the city.
* Brother Kai is seeking enlightenment after being sent out from his monastery.
* Floyd GoldSeeker survived the Glittervein mine collapse after a golden ray of light gave him strength to escape; he believes this was divine intervention.
* Floyd GoldSeeker reached Level 2 at the Clifftop Observatory after hearing: "You were not the only one buried. Dig."
* Sparkrender was trying to mine the power of dead dragons at the Clifftop Observatory.
* The party attacked Sparkrender immediately upon reaching him in D5.
* Pat Benatar struck Sparkrender hard with her shortbow.
* Throk killed Sparkrender with Eldritch Blast.
* Sparkrender is dead.
* Another dragon, Aidron, remains trapped beneath or inside the Clifftop Observatory tower and has not yet been encountered or freed.
* The Observatory ritual, gathered dragon power, D5 puzzle, hoard, lower route, and D6 remain unresolved.
