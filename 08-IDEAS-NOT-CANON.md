## Possible Campaign Premise - Not Yet Canon

* The world has suffered, or will suffer, a major catastrophe that weakens the barriers between planes.
* One possible cause is the destruction of a powerful artifact tied to Chaos or Entropy, causing instability across realms.
* The ancient polytheistic pantheons may be under siege by a viral, ultra-violent monotheistic faith.
* As gods and followers fall, planar barriers may erode further, allowing entropy and chaos to spread.
