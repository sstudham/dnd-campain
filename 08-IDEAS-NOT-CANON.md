# Ideas Not Canon

Everything in this file is campaign planning, not established world truth. Preserve player ownership of unresolved character mysteries and do not move answers into canon unless Scott explicitly approves them as canon.

## Possible Campaign Premise — The Concordance

### Core Concept

* Reality consists of many planes, timelines, and alternate realities that are different but normally compatible.
* The multiverse is not held together by sameness.
* It is held together by a system tentatively called the Concordance.
* The Concordance allows different histories, gods, forms of magic, and physical laws to coexist without collapsing into one another.
* The existing rift remains a wound in reality.
* The original cause of the wound remains unknown.
* Acererak did not necessarily create the wound.
* Acererak may have discovered the damaged system, learned to exploit it, and begun accelerating its drift toward a future in which he becomes the single constant shared by all realities.
* Acererak is the leading proposed central antagonist, not established canon, until the campaign group accepts that direction.

### Concordance Terminology

* Concordance:
  * The overall system that keeps planes, timelines, and realities compatible.
  * It permits meaningful difference without total collapse.
* Concordance Anchor:
  * A location, structure, ecosystem, creature, or phenomenon that performs a major stabilizing function.
  * Possible Anchors include ancient cities, sacred trees, dragon burial sites, celestial mechanisms, soul passages, libraries, living beings, or entire pocket domains.
* Heartstone:
  * A physical core found inside some Anchors.
  * Not every Anchor has a literal Heartstone.
  * Avoid turning the campaign into a collection of identical magical rocks.
* Relay:
  * A smaller site that carries, redirects, or distributes resonance between major Anchors.
  * Stormwreck Isle may contain or sit near a minor draconic Relay.
* Instrument:
  * A device used to observe, measure, or assist with tuning an Anchor or Relay.
  * Instruments are not necessarily Anchors.
  * The Clifftop Observatory's golden astronomical sculpture may be an ancient diagnostic Instrument.
  * The Harmonic Regulator may be a portable or semi-portable Instrument.
* Artificial receiver:
  * A constructed object that captures displaced magical, divine, or planar energy without repairing the Concordance.
  * The Cult of the One God's worshiping stones may be artificial receivers.
  * These stones may catch prayer intended for other gods and redirect it into an unknown system.

### Effects of Concordance Instability

* When the Concordance drifts, realities cease to remain properly compatible.
* Possible symptoms include:
  * Space opening into the wrong location.
  * Multiple places occupying the same physical area.
  * Time remembering incompatible sequences.
  * People recalling lives they did not live.
  * History changing without everyone noticing.
  * Prayer reaching the wrong god, plane, or receiver.
  * Divine magic becoming inconsistent.
  * Arcane magic temporarily following another reality's rules.
  * Souls losing their proper path after death.
  * Resurrected people disappearing from memory.
  * Undead remaining caught between possible endings.
  * Maps, stars, and planar routes changing.
  * Creatures, objects, or landscapes appearing from alternate histories.
  * Different versions of the same person briefly existing at once.

### Retuning an Anchor

* A true retuning requires three elements:
  * Place.
  * History.
  * Living Choice.
* Place:
  * The party must determine where or how the Anchor properly belongs within reality.
  * Place may involve geography, planar position, cultural identity, celestial alignment, or the proper relationship among multiple locations.
* History:
  * The party must determine what genuinely happened.
  * A stable History may require restoring erased events, resolving deliberate lies, or preserving more than one legitimate account.
  * History should not always mean discovering one objectively correct story.
  * Some Anchors may stabilize only when multiple conflicting but meaningful histories are allowed to coexist.
* Living Choice:
  * People who must live with the restored Anchor must participate in determining what it becomes.
  * A legitimate Choice must be informed, voluntary, locally grounded, and broad enough to represent those who bear its consequences.
  * Coercion, captured souls, manufactured unanimity, or a ruler's unilateral decree cannot complete a true retuning.
  * Powerful outsiders cannot legitimately decide everything for another world.
  * A lich, construct, or dead civilization may understand the system but cannot supply the necessary living consent.
  * Alternate-character adventures may focus on local heroes making the Choice for their own reality.
  * Acererak may counterfeit consensus through the Soulmonger or other captured souls, but the result remains incomplete and unstable while genuine alternatives survive.

### Retuning Rules

* Properly retuned Anchors remain stable.
* Acererak cannot casually switch them off again.
* Reversing a retuning requires a major attack on its Place, History, or Living Choice.
* Possible attacks include:
  * Erasing the witnesses who established its History.
  * Coercing or deceiving its population into rejecting the earlier Choice.
  * Physically or planarly displacing the Anchor.
  * Replacing restored history with a new structural lie.
* Player victories must remain meaningful.
* Restored regions should show permanent improvements.
* Possible improvements include:
  * Reliable prayer.
  * Fewer undead disturbances.
  * Stable maps and routes.
  * Returned memories.
  * Safer planar travel.
  * Healing environments.
  * New allies and access points.
  * Local magical benefits.

### Dissonance, Echoes, and Ethical Repair

* Dissonance is normally non-conscious structural residue produced when incompatible realities grind against one another.
* It may contain magical pressure, broken causal patterns, abandoned outcomes, contradictory memories, or unrealized possibilities.
* A conscious person, persistent alternate self, or self-aware historical Echo is not ordinary dissonance.
* Retuning must not casually erase conscious Echoes.
* Conscious Echoes require rescue, integration, relocation, coexistence, or their own informed Living Choice.
* Successful retuning sharply reduces the continuing production of dissonance.
* A retuning may create one brief purge of accumulated non-conscious dissonance.
* The heroes' repairs are always a net improvement.
* The campaign must not reveal that their earlier victories were mistakes.
* Acererak may control a system that intercepts:
  * Ambient dissonance from unstable Anchors.
  * Concentrated purges released during retuning.
  * Conscious Echoes he has deliberately captured rather than protected.
* The Soulmonger may eventually be revealed as the primary reservoir for collected dissonance and imprisoned Echoes.
* At approximately Level 14, the party may discover a grounding method that prevents theft from deliberate future retunings.
* Grounding is not unlimited:
  * It requires preparation and a known Anchor.
  * It cannot automatically contain every remote catastrophe.
  * A vast uncontrolled event, such as a failed Tiamat ritual, may exceed its capacity or release a different kind of dissonance.
* Earlier Anchors remain successfully repaired even if Acererak stole their purges.

### Acererak — Proposed Central Antagonist

* Acererak is not necessarily the original creator of the rift.
* He may be the primary force accelerating the crisis and directing it toward a specific outcome.
* Acererak first discovers:
  * Omu's soul-related Anchor.
  * The measurable resonance of souls.
  * Structural residue produced by Concordance instability.
  * Methods for storing dissonance and captured Echoes.
* Present-day Acererak independently begins this research.
* He later identifies a synchronization signal traveling backward through time.
* The signal matches his own soul-signature.
* The source is a possible future in which Acererak has already succeeded.

### Acererak's Motivation

* Acererak has already escaped ordinary mortality.
* His deeper enemy is contingency.
* In an infinite multiverse:
  * Some realities contain his defeat.
  * Some contain his destruction.
  * Some forget him.
  * Some never include him.
* Acererak considers every victory incomplete while another possible reality contradicts it.
* He concludes that infinite possibility creates instability and eventual collapse.
* He believes reality requires one universal reference point.
* He intends to become that reference point.
* Possible titles for this state:
  * The Invariant.
  * The Final Measure.
  * The Still Point.

### The Invariant

* If Acererak becomes the Invariant:
  * Every viable timeline must include him.
  * Every death and resurrection must obey laws defined by him.
  * Every planar route must use coordinates permitted by him.
  * Prayer and divine contact may pass through a system calibrated to him.
  * Contradictory histories may collapse into one permitted account.
  * Realities in which he loses may become impossible.
* Acererak would not become a normal god.
* He would become a law of existence.
* His multiversal tomb would not be one dungeon.
* Reality itself would become his dungeon:
  * Every entrance controlled.
  * Every exit anticipated.
  * Every death categorized.
  * Every possible outcome trapped.

### The Growing Multiversal Tomb

* Acererak's intercepted dissonance may become architecture inside a tomb that does not fully exist yet.
* Each major theft may add another impossible corridor, locked door, trap, crypt, or chamber.
* Fragments of this structure may appear in:
  * Dreams.
  * Damaged maps.
  * Instrument readings.
  * Alternate realities.
  * Rooms that briefly overlap unrelated dungeons.
* Repeated geometry provides evidence of a single collector before Acererak is named.
* The structure should look increasingly like a tomb built out of stolen possibilities.
* By Level 20, the revelation that reality is becoming Acererak's dungeon should feel like the completion of a pattern the party has been seeing for years.

### Backward Signal and Time Loop

* A future Invariant version of Acererak sends a synchronization signal backward through time.
* The signal pulls unstable Anchors toward Acererak's frequency.
* Properly retuned and grounded Anchors resist it.
* This produces a self-reinforcing loop:
  * Present Acererak discovers the damaged Concordance.
  * He begins harvesting dissonance.
  * He detects the future signal and recognizes himself.
  * He deliberately creates the conditions required to become its source.
* The signal does not create present Acererak's original ambition.
* Present Acererak chooses to pursue the future after discovering it.
* Killing present Acererak's body cannot by itself erase the future signal.
* The party must make the future in which he becomes the Invariant structurally impossible.

### Acererak's Limits

* Acererak does not control every local villain.
* Local villains retain independent motives.
* Sparkrender is exploiting Stormwreck for his own ambition.
* The Cult of the Dragon believes it serves draconic goals.
* The Cult of the One God follows its own religious interpretation.
* Halaster uses Concordance machinery for his own reasons.
* Local villains may never know Acererak exists.
* Acererak may:
  * Purchase research.
  * Leak information.
  * Encourage dangerous experiments.
  * Harvest the consequences of failed rituals.
  * Manipulate which choices are available during a crisis.
* Acererak should not be the secret employer behind every antagonist.
* He cannot complete a true retuning without legitimate Living Choice, which is why he instead tries to eliminate every alternative.
* He does not specifically require the current party.
* The party becomes uniquely dangerous after learning how to:
  * Retune Anchors.
  * Preserve multiple truths.
  * Ground dissonance.
  * Build a network of voluntary allies.

### Cult of the One God

* The Cult of the One God remains independent from Acererak.
* It is not the true central antagonist.
* Its artificial worshiping stones capture displaced prayer.
* The cult interprets the distant Invariant frequency as evidence that:
  * All gods are becoming one.
  * One god is replacing the old pantheons.
  * Religious disagreement is ending.
* The cult is wrong about the source but correctly senses a singular influence.
* Individual members may include:
  * Sincere believers.
  * Frightened converts.
  * Opportunists.
  * Political manipulators.
  * Researchers.
  * People whose prayers genuinely stopped receiving familiar answers.
* A recurring sincere believer should give the cult a human face:
  * Their worshiping stone genuinely restored comfort, healing, or a sense that prayer was heard.
  * Disabling the receiver solves a structural danger but creates a human need.
  * The party may need to provide a safer spiritual or communal alternative.
  * The believer may become an ally, critic, reformer, or tragic opponent depending on the party's choices.
* The cult foreshadows Acererak's philosophy:
  * One god.
  * One truth.
  * One law.
  * One history.
  * One permitted reality.

### Philosophical Positions

* Restorers:
  * Want to return the Concordance to its previous harmonies.
  * May treat the ancient system as sacred or inherently correct.
* Recomposers:
  * Believe the old Concordance is no longer sufficient.
  * Want living civilizations to create a new arrangement.
* Exploiters:
  * Use instability for magic, immortality, empire, religious power, or wealth.
* The Invariant:
  * Believes many realities must collapse into one stable universal frequency.
  * Acererak intends to become that frequency.
* The campaign's eventual final decision should emerge through earlier choices.
* Introduce the consequences of restoration, change, exploitation, and imposed order long before Level 20.

### Stormwreck Connection

* Stormwreck Isle may sit near a minor draconic Relay.
* Dragon deaths have saturated the island with resonance.
* This allows the same wound to manifest as:
  * Fire.
  * Fumes.
  * Blight.
  * Unnatural growth.
  * Undeath.
  * Memory.
  * Storms.
  * Awakened dragon power.
* The Clifftop Observatory's golden astronomical sculpture may be an Instrument.
* It measures:
  * Timing.
  * Pressure.
  * Celestial movement.
  * Dragon death.
  * Hidden lines of power.
* Sparkrender is trying to mine the dead dragons' power.
* He is not knowingly operating the Concordance.
* The Instrument may briefly detect a rhythm coming from a future location or moment that should not yet exist.
* Do not explain Acererak or the Invariant during Stormwreck.
* Stormwreck should teach only that:
  * The different anomalies are connected.
  * Ancient people or dragons studied the connection.
  * Something beyond the island is responding.
* Stabilizing Stormwreck should end its acute escalation without erasing its ancient dragon-death scars, history, or future usefulness as a home base.

### Alternate-Reality Adventures

* Some Anchors can only be retuned by native inhabitants.
* These stories may use temporary player characters.
* Alternate characters may begin with different base skills or magical assumptions.
* Possible alternate magical systems:
  * Magic requires communal consent.
  * Memory replaces spell slots.
  * Divine magic does not function.
  * Music creates spell effects.
  * Dragons rule and traditional divine classes are forbidden.
  * Time flows backward.
  * Death behaves differently.
* Alternate characters make decisions for their own worlds.
* They are not tools or summoned assistants for the main party.
* They may later return as allies, rivals, leaders, legends, or displaced versions of themselves.
* These side adventures may support rotating DMs.
