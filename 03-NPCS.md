# NPCs

## Allies / Contacts

* Shorn - caravan leader or merchant connected to the Blackford Road caravan.
* Captain Braddok Ironhand - dwarf captain tied to Severed Whisper's arrival from the Tabaxi homeland and Luskan history.
* Captain Elric Thorne - captain of The Gull's Mercy; helped carry the party to Stormwreck Isle after Pat Benatar worked her dock contacts; irritated by Pat Benatar and Kai using breath weapons on his boat.
* Elder Runara - calm leader at Dragon's Rest; knows more than she says. DM-only secrets should stay labeled DM-only. Canonical Session 15 ending: she reunites with Aidron, tells the party that what awakened in the Observatory reached outward, and directs them to follow the dragons if they want to understand the wound.
* Tarak - Dragon's Rest healer/herbalist; practical, kind, and tired.
* Varnoth - protective Dragon's Rest resident with a soldier's bearing; suspicious but defensive of the cloister.
* Myla - Dragon's Rest kobold; her brothers Mek and Minn were at the Clifftop Observatory. Canonical Session 15 ending: she sees Minn carrying Mek home, understands he is dead, and joins the kobolds in receiving his body.
* Minn - Myla's surviving brother. The party gave him 1 gp after the D2 stirge fight, making him extremely cooperative. He shared everything he knew and offered to introduce the party safely to the kobold camp. He asked the party to return Mek's body to Dragon's Rest. The party gave him another 1 gp to remain near the magical bridge and watch it while they explored D4 and D5. Canonical Session 15 ending: Minn carries Mek back to Dragon's Rest, remains until the others have finished grieving, and says, "He came home." He stands with Myla as the party leaves Stormwreck Isle.

## Rivals

* Grusk - orc attacker from the Blackford Road ambush; escaped.
* Morghul - orc attacker from the Blackford Road ambush; escaped.
* Sparkrender - young blue dragon who was exploiting dead-dragon power at the Clifftop Observatory. The party attacked him immediately in D5. Pat Benatar struck him hard with her shortbow, and Throk killed him with Eldritch Blast. Sparkrender is dead.

## Unknown or Unsorted

* Bagronk War-Scarred - Throk's father, a regional orc commander who shifted from raider to protector of trade routes.
* Grek - Throk's younger sister.
* Mek - Myla's brother; died during the D2 Rotunda Ruins stirge encounter at the Clifftop Observatory. Canonical Session 15 ending: Minn returns Mek's body to Dragon's Rest and the kobolds gather around him without ceremony or speech.
* Aidron - living bronze dragon trapped below D5 of the Clifftop Observatory in shifting stone and corrupted golden lines. Canonical Session 15 ending: the party frees him; he returns alive to Dragon's Rest and reunites with Runara. Aidron confirms that other dragons will have felt what awakened in the Observatory, warns that some dragons will lie or try to use it, and cautions the party not to assume dragons should be trusted.
