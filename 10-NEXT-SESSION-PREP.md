## Scenes

### Scene 1 - Temple Aftermath

The worshiping stone is split.
Silver-blue rift light pulses from the crack.
Freed worshipers are scared, ashamed, and confused.
The two dragonborn hear one word: "Stormwreck."

Checks:

* Arcana/Religion DC 12: the stone was not giving power to a god; it was pulling prayer into something else.
* History/Religion DC 14: "old dragon bones make thin places" sounds like ancient draconic magic.

Read/describe:
The chanting stops all at once. The rings of worshipers break apart like people waking from the same nightmare. The cracked altar leaks silver-blue light. For one heartbeat, the split stone shows black waves, jagged rocks, drowned hands, and a ruined observatory on a cliff.
To the dragonborn only: a word rolls through your bones. "Stormwreck."

Dialogue:

* Freed cultist: "I remember chanting. I remember giving something away. Not gold. Not blood. Prayer."
* Freed cultist: "There was no god. There was something wrong behind the prayer."
* Old worshiper: "They said old dragon bones make thin places in the world."
* Young worshiper: "The monk went north. He said if the rift opened again, look for Dragon's Rest."

NPCs:

AC 10, HP 4, Speed 30
Stats: Commoner
Use: scared witness
Knows: worshipers were promised "one god above all gods."

Brother Pell, broken cult speaker
AC 12, HP 9, Speed 30
Attack: dagger +3, 1d4+1
Use: guilty but useful
Knows: the monk said "Dragon's Rest" before leaving.

Old Salla, half-aware worshiper
AC 10, HP 4, Speed 25
Stats: Commoner
Use: creepy prophecy voice
Knows: "The sea is full of bones that still dream."

### Scene 2 - First Rest / Tavern

Let the party eat, rest, and talk.
A courier enters looking for Pat Benatar.
The box opens only for Pat Benatar.
Both dragonborn can make it hum.
The note points them to Dragon's Rest.

Read/describe:
The tavern is warm, loud, and normal in a way the ruined temple was not. Rain taps the shutters. The fire smells like pine. Then the door opens, and a wet courier steps in holding a small sealed box.

Dialogue:
Courier: "Pat Benatar?"
Courier: "I was paid to give this to a dragonborn who didn't look like a dragonborn."
Courier: "No offense. Those were the exact words."
Courier: "Don't ask me by who. Hooded woman. Bronze eyes. Paid in old coin."

Box:
The box smells of sea salt. Both dragonborn feel drawn to it.

Note:
"Dragon's Rest. Stormwreck Isle. Ask Runara. The rift is older than your clan."

When opened:
The scale warms in Pat Benatar's hand. If either dragonborn touches it, it hums like a tuning fork. All dragonborn see blue lightning, a bronze dragon eye, drowned sailors, and the same cracked worshiping stone lying underwater.

NPCs:
Merrin Quickstep, dock courier
AC 13, HP 7, Speed 30

Use: nervous delivery person
Knows: paid by bronze-eyed hooded woman.

Olma Reed, tavern keeper
AC 10, HP 5, Speed 25
Stats: Commoner
Use: grounding NPC
Knows: sailors have been avoiding Stormwreck waters.

Garrick Vale, drunk local
AC 10, HP 6, Speed 30

### Scene 3 - Pat Benatar Works the Docks

Purpose:
Let Pat Benatar shine as a well-known shipping logistics person with port contacts, practical knowledge, and a reputation for getting cargo moved.

Beat:

* The party needs passage to Stormwreck Isle.
* Pat Benatar knows the shipping lanes, cargo schedules, and which captains will take quiet jobs without asking too many questions.
* She identifies Captain Elric Thorne of The Gull's Mercy as the best option.
* Elric is separate from Captain Braddok Ironhand.
* Braddok remains tied to Severed Whisper and Luskan.
* Elric helps because Pat Benatar once got his cargo released from a dockmaster's hold by finding a missing customs seal and proving the paperwork was valid.

Read-aloud:
Pat Benatar does not wander the docks like a tourist. She moves like someone reading a ledger written in rope, tide, cargo marks, and nervous sailors. Within minutes, she knows which ships are leaving, which captains are lying, and which one might be desperate or loyal enough to risk Stormwreck waters.
Then she spots The Gull's Mercy.
Its captain, Elric Thorne, sees Pat Benatar coming and immediately looks tired.
"Whatever this is," he says, "I already hate it."

Elric dialogue:

* "Stormwreck? That's ugly water right now. But if you're asking, I assume this is worse than ugly."
* "Tiny island. Dragon's Rest cloister. Healers, monks, hermits. Strange place."
* "Lately? Bad waters. Wreck lights. Dead things walking the beach."
* "I'll take you if you guard the cargo and don't make me regret knowing you."

NPC:
Captain Elric Thorne

* AC 12, HP 16, Speed 30
* Attack: scimitar +4, 1d6+2
* Use: practical sailor and Pat Benatar contact
* Trait: brave until supernatural things happen
* Ship: The Gull's Mercy

### Scene 4 - Boat Travel Encounter

Night fog rolls in.
Ghostly cult chanting rises from nowhere.
Cargo breaks loose.
One undead sailor climbs aboard.
The scale points toward Stormwreck like a compass.
Run fast: 2-3 rounds, then scene ends.

Skill challenge:
Need 3 successes before 2 failures.

Options:

* Athletics DC 12: secure cargo.
* Acrobatics DC 12: cross slick deck.
* Survival DC 12: read waves/wind.
* Perception DC 13: spot the undead before it climbs aboard.
* Religion/Arcana DC 13: disrupt the chanting.
* Sleight of Hand DC 12: knot ropes fast.

Failure effects:

* 1 failure: cargo smashes; someone makes Dex save DC 12 or falls prone.
* 2 failures: one supply crate lost; Captain is irritated but keeps sailing.
* Big success: Captain respects them; free passage.

Undead sailor entrance:
A dead hand slaps the rail. A soaked sailor drags himself over the side, jaw hanging loose, eyes full of blue light.

Dialogue/whispers:
Ghostly chanting: "One god. One wound. One sky torn open."
Undead sailor: "Not a god."
Undead sailor: "The world is splitting."
Then it collapses.

NPCs:
Captain Elric Thorne
AC 12, HP 16
Attack: scimitar +4, 1d6+2
Use: yells orders, does not steal spotlight.

Pip Nackle
AC 11, HP 5
Attack: club +2, 1d4
Use: gets pinned by sliding cargo unless helped.

Drowned Sailor
AC 8, HP 12, Speed 20
Attack: slam +3, 1d6+1 bludgeoning
Traits: undead; immune poison
Fast rule: skip Undead Fortitude unless you want a harder fight.

### Scene 5 - Arrival at Stormwreck Isle

The scale grows hot.
Stormwreck Isle appears.
Dragon's Rest is visible above black rocks.
A bell rings.
Someone screams from the beach.
Start the official module's arrival/beach trouble.

Read:
Black rocks rise from gray water. Above them, a peaceful cloister sits against the cliffs. Farther inland, broken stone points toward the sky like the ribs of something ancient.
The scale in Pat Benatar's hand grows hot.
A bell rings from Dragon's Rest.
Then someone screams from the beach.

Dialogue:
Captain Elric: "That's Dragon's Rest."
Pip: "Please tell me screams are normal there."
Voice from shore: "Help! By all the gods, help!"

Optional Runara glimpse:
An elderly woman in simple robes stands high above the beach, watching the storm with impossible calm. For one breath, lightning makes her shadow look like wings.
