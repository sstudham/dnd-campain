## Tonight in Plain English - What They Are Doing and Why

The party came to the Clifftop Observatory because Runara believes it is the source of the danger now gathering above Stormwreck Isle. The party has already learned that a young blue dragon named Sparkrender is using the Observatory's ancient machinery to draw power from all the dragons that have died on the island. He is not studying that power; he is trying to take it for himself.

Sparkrender captured Aidron, a young bronze dragon from Dragon's Rest, and imprisoned him beneath the main tower. Sparkrender intends to use Aidron as the living sacrifice or final key in a ritual that will bind the dead dragons' power to him. If the party does nothing, Aidron dies and Sparkrender becomes much more dangerous.

Tonight the party is trying to find the way into the tower, rescue Aidron, and stop Sparkrender's ritual. D4 is an abandoned study containing the clue and a Potion of Lightning Resistance. D5 is the main tower, where Sparkrender is sleeping above a magical star map and four movable statues. Solving that statue puzzle opens the hidden staircase to D6, where Aidron is trapped.

The clean story for tonight is:

1. Recover from the stirge fight and speak with Minn.
2. Deal with Sparkrender's kobolds without necessarily fighting them.
3. Explore D4 and discover how to open the hidden staircase.
4. Sneak past or confront Sparkrender in D5.
5. Reach D6 and free Aidron.
6. Fight Sparkrender with Aidron's help, either tonight or next session.

The central player choice is simple: attack Sparkrender immediately, or risk sneaking past him so they can rescue Aidron and return with an ally.

## Opening Narration - Read Aloud

Last time, the morning began in the shelter of Dragon's Rest.

Runara sent you toward the Clifftop Observatory with a warning. The sickness beneath Stormwreck Isle had shown itself in the fumes and fire of Seagrow Caves. The dead around the island had shown themselves aboard the Compass Rose. Whatever was gathering above the island was waiting among the ruined towers.

Before you left, Runara placed a moonstone key in Floyd GoldSeeker's hands.

The path crossed black basalt split by glassy growths and red, branching scars. Sparkrender passed overhead, a streak of blue wings and distant thunder. He did not see you. Floyd saw something else: stone struck from above, but broken from within.

At the cliff's edge, two marble dragons waited. The moonstone key woke the gold in their veins, and a bridge of light reached across empty air.

For Floyd, the cliff became Glittervein again.

Darkness. Falling stone. The silence after the last pickaxe stopped. The golden ray that found him when no one else could.

And a voice:

“You were not spared to find gold. You were spared to find the way out.”

Across the bridge, the Observatory did not welcome you. Eight stirges descended on the ruined rotunda where two kobold brothers were already fighting for their lives.

Every one of you killed at least one.

Mek did not survive.

Minn did.

Severed Whisper drove his blade through the final creature and said, “I would like to introduce myself. I am sorry...”

Then the five dragon effigies around the golden instrument began to make sense: bronze, gold, brass, blue, and red. The blue light was not studying the others. It was forcing them into alignment.

Floyd heard pickaxes again. Not one. Many.

His fallen crew stood around him in memory, rhythm, and grief. His holy symbol burned gold. His warhammer became the old mining tool in his hands.

“You were not the only one buried. Dig.”

Now the stirges lie dead across the Observatory floor. Mek lies still beside the instrument. Minn kneels over his brother, too shocked to speak.

The blue light turns once around the golden rings.

From somewhere inside the tower comes a heavy crash.

Dust spills from the stones beneath your feet.

Something is alive down there.

And somewhere above it, thunder is breathing.

What do you do?

## Scene 1 - The Rotunda After the Stirges

### What the Scene Is For

* Let the session breathe before introducing another threat.

### Description

The golden sculpture continues its slow motion, but it no longer feels like a model of the heavens. Each ring turns with a faint metallic strain. The blue light advances in sharp pulses. The bronze light flickers and pulls downward, toward the base of the tallest tower.

Minn is beside Mek. He keeps placing both hands against his brother's chest as if the correct pressure might restart him.

From the tower comes another impact: stone against stone, followed by a muffled, furious growl.

### Minn

Play Minn as shocked, guilty, and eager to be useful because usefulness is easier than grief.

What he knows:

* Sparkrender ordered the kobolds to build and arrange the five dragon effigies.
* Sparkrender has been waiting for the golden instrument to show that the time is right.
* A bronze-colored dragon arrived several days ago and argued with Sparkrender.
* The bronze dragon has been trapped below the main tower ever since.
* Sparkrender ordered everyone to leave the new cave-in at the tower's base untouched.
* Other kobolds are camped in D3.
* An old study stands alone across another gap at D4.

Useful Minn lines:

* “Mek said the blue one would make us important.”
* “The other dragon is still making noise. That means he is still alive.”
* “Sparkrender said not to move the fallen wall. He said the stone was doing its job.”
* “If Myla asks... I do not know what I am supposed to tell her.”

Do not make Minn blame the party. He can be angry, but Sparkrender's orders and the danger of the Observatory are the true pressure.

### Player Opportunities

* Floyd can examine the tower and recognize a deliberate secondary collapse: someone weakened the wall from outside to trap whatever was below.
* Pat Benatar can read that Minn is holding back fear of Sparkrender, not secret loyalty.
* Brother Kai can choose whether to offer comfort, practical help, silence, or distance. Do not force a moral lesson.
* Throk can frame strength as getting the living home rather than avenging the dead.
* Severed Whisper can identify the safest routes among D3, D4, and D5.

### Recovery

If the players discuss resting, tell them plainly that the rotunda is defensible for about an hour. Minn can warn them that the ritual feels close, but not so close that a short rest is reckless.

## Scene 2 - D3 Kobold Camp

### First Impression

A rope-and-driftwood bridge reaches a patched ruin crowded with bedrolls, baskets, polished sling stones, and scraps of blue cloth. Five kobolds freeze when the party appears: Ekrash, Erp, Hev, and the winged kobolds Nuhro and Snirke.

Their first response is defensive noise, not an immediate attack.

### How to Run the Conversation

The RAW kobolds are hostile and loyal to Sparkrender. In this campaign, Minn and Mek's death make the scene more complicated.

* If Minn accompanies the party and speaks first, shift the camp from hostile to frightened and suspicious.
* Mentioning Myla also matters; these kobolds know she is alive and connected to Dragon's Rest.
* Ekrash is the loud loyalist. He repeats Sparkrender's promises because admitting doubt would make Mek's death feel meaningless.
* Nuhro watches exits and is the first to believe the party may actually rescue the trapped dragon.
* Snirke wants permission to survive without being called a traitor.
* Erp and Hev follow whichever voice sounds most certain.

Use the RAW social benchmarks if dice are needed: DC 13 for a forceful Intimidation approach, or roughly DC 15 for Deception or Persuasion against active hostility. Lower the difficulty or skip the roll when Minn vouches for the party and the players address the kobolds' actual fears.

Information the party can gain:

* Sparkrender is sleeping in the upper tower while he waits.
* The metallic dragon is beneath him.
* The D4 study contains old books Sparkrender could not open.
* The moonstone key can awaken the second bridge from D2 to D4.
* A winged kobold can retrieve the key if it was left in the D1 bridge anchor.
* Sparkrender wants the kobolds present for the ritual, but they do not know the ritual may kill Aidron.

Success does not need to turn the kobolds into combat allies. A good outcome is that they stand aside, fetch the key, keep watch, or promise not to warn Sparkrender.

## Scene 3 - D4 Isolated Study

### Reaching It

The gap is 22 feet. The clean route is to use the moonstone key in D2 and create the second energy bridge. If the key remained in D1, Nuhro or Snirke can retrieve it. If the players established that Floyd kept the key, accept that table state and continue.

Reward ropes, grappling hooks, climbing, swimming, jumping, or other workable ideas. The point is to let the party feel clever, not to enforce a single route.

### Read Aloud

The bridge carries you to a cramped tower open to the wind. Waves beat far below through a hole where half the floor has fallen away. Collapsed shelves lean over drifts of ruined paper, but one small black journal remains intact beneath a slab of marble. Its lock is clean. Everything around it is not.

### The Journal

Use the RAW challenge:

* DC 15 Perception notices the arcane trap on the lock.
* DC 11 Arcana explains how to spoil the rune with a careful scratch.
* After the rune is disabled, DC 10 Dexterity with thieves' tools opens the lock, or DC 12 Strength breaks it.
* Opening the lock without disarming the rune releases noxious gas for 1d6 poison damage to the opener.

The clue can be delivered in campaign language:

“Four scholars turn their eyes toward the Dragon of Dawn. Where their sight meets, the descent into hidden knowledge begins.”

This points to the four statues and the dragon-shaped constellation in D5.

### Campaign-Specific Details

The journal's authors treated the golden mechanism as an instrument for listening to the island's dragon-death energy. A later hand changed the language from listening to drawing, then from drawing to taking. Do not name the Concordance or explain the rift.

If Pat Benatar handles the journal near the sculpture, her clan-marked scale becomes briefly warm. It offers recognition, not an answer.

If Floyd studies the tower, he spots a loose brick because its weight sits incorrectly against the wall. Behind it are the RAW Potion of Lightning Resistance and 10 gp.

## Scene 4 - D5 Observatory Tower

### Read Aloud

The upper tower rises above the rest of the ruins. Broken stained glass scatters bruised colors over a star map worked into the floor with gold and tiny stones. Four enormous scholar statues stand around its edge, each facing a different part of the sky.

In the northeast corner, curled around coins and blue gems, sleeps Sparkrender.

Each slow breath makes lightning crawl between his horns.

Directly beneath him, something strikes the tower wall once.

Sparkrender's eye opens halfway, then closes.

### Make the Choice Obvious

Tell the players what their characters can plainly understand:

* They can attack the sleeping dragon.
* They can try to move quietly through the room and solve the statue mechanism.
* They can climb down and clear the caved-in wall from outside.
* They can retreat and invent another plan.

Do not conceal the rescue route behind a roll. Checks decide cost, noise, and speed, not whether the players are allowed to understand their options.

### RAW Mechanics to Keep Handy

* Group movement past Sparkrender: at least half the party succeeds on DC 14 Stealth.
* Turning the north and east statues near him requires separate DC 14 Stealth checks to avoid waking him.
* DC 10 Investigation finds the dragon-shaped constellation in the southeast part of the star map.
* DC 15 Perception notices turning grooves on the statues.
* While turning a statue, DC 10 Perception notices it settle when aimed correctly.
* When all four point to the Dragon of Dawn, the floor lowers into a spiral stair to D6.
* A character speaking Draconic can delay immediate violence with a DC 12 Deception, Intimidation, or Persuasion check. Mentioning Runara or Aidron ends Sparkrender's patience.

### Sparkrender's Voice

Play him as vain, young, intelligent enough to be dangerous, and deeply insecure about being small.

He does not think he is desecrating the dead. He thinks the dead wasted power by failing to take it with them.

Useful ideas:

* He calls the dead dragons “ore left in the ground.”
* He calls Aidron “the final conductor” or “the living key.”
* He sees Runara's restraint as cowardice.
* He is fascinated by Floyd's survival and immediately draws the wrong conclusion: that survival proves superiority.
* He may offer the party a place beneath him if they help complete the ritual.

Do not turn this into a long villain monologue unless the players keep him talking to buy time.

### Combat Reminder

Sparkrender is AC 17 with 52 HP. His lightning breath is a 30-foot line, DC 12 Dexterity save, 4d10 lightning damage, recharging on 5-6. The bite is +5 and deals piercing plus lightning damage. He is immune to lightning.

Use the room:

* Statues provide cover from a line breath.
* Lightning crawls across the floor before a charged breath, telegraphing the danger.
* Sparkrender wants to dominate the center of the room and force the party to line up.
* In the normal tower encounter, he attempts to flee at 10 HP or fewer.

## Scene 5 - D6 Secret Library and Aidron

### Entry Options

The party can descend through the statue mechanism or clear the outside cave-in.

Clearing the rubble takes one person about 30 minutes, two people about 15 minutes, or the whole party about 6 minutes. Quiet work takes twice as long and calls for the same group DC 14 Stealth benchmark used near Sparkrender.

Floyd should be especially good at identifying which stones can be moved safely, but he should not be mechanically required. His value is letting the group work with confidence and without triggering another collapse.

### Read Aloud

The stair turns beneath the tower and opens into stale air, old parchment, and broken glass. Shelves cover the walls from floor to ceiling, their books swollen by damp and age.

A desk explodes into splinters at the far end of the room.

From the wreckage rises a bronze dragon no larger than a bear. Dust coats his scales. One wing hangs low. His claws are bloodied from digging.

He looks at the open stair, then at you.

“Did Runara send you?”

Above, thunder answers for you.

### Aidron

Aidron is proud, frightened, exhausted, and ashamed that Sparkrender defeated him.

What he tells the party:

* He came to confront Sparkrender because he believed Runara was being too cautious.
* Sparkrender beat him and sealed him in the archive.
* Sparkrender plans to use Aidron's death to seize the power of Stormwreck's dead dragons and become something much stronger.
* The timing depends on the movement shown by the golden instrument.
* Aidron wants to face Sparkrender immediately, but he can listen to a plan if the party treats him as an ally rather than a rescued child.

Keep Aidron's self-reproach focused on his choices. He should not become another authority who explains the campaign's full mystery.

### Library Rewards and One Eerie Clue

Use the RAW reward: a successful DC 15 Investigation check, Detect Magic, or Aidron's cataloging reveals either a +1 battleaxe or a Hold Person spell scroll.

Optional campaign clue: one damaged star chart records a measurement that appeared a fraction of a heartbeat before the celestial event that caused it. The margin simply says, “The answer arrived before the question.” This is evidence that the Observatory detected something impossible, not an explanation.

## Strong Session Endings

### Ending A - The Sleeping Dragon

Use if the party reaches D5 late.

End when the party sees Sparkrender asleep above the star map and hears Aidron strike the wall beneath him. Ask each player, in one sentence, whether their character's first instinct is attack, rescue, bargain, or retreat. Cut before the plan begins.

### Ending B - The Dragon Beneath

Use if the party frees Aidron near the end.

Aidron looks up as blue light leaks through the ceiling. The golden instrument outside accelerates. He says, “He has started without me. That means he is afraid of you.”

End there.

### Ending C - Roll Initiative

Use if the table moves quickly.

Let the party return to D5 with Aidron. Sparkrender wakes, sees the bronze dragon free, and realizes his ritual is slipping away. End after initiative is rolled and the first arc of lightning crosses the star map.

Do not feel obligated to complete the combat and Stormwreck aftermath in the same night. The final battle deserves room.

* Dragon-spirit effects occur at the end of each round.

This is a consequence, not a punishment. The players chose safety, and Sparkrender used the time.
